Our services

Everything a field team actually needs.

01

One vault, every discipline

Every report, photo, video, and graph a teammate posts lands in one shared vault, organized by expedition rather than by whoever happened to upload it. Photos keep their EXIF data, videos keep their clip tags, PDF reports become fully searchable, and telemetry graphs render straight from the raw CSV or JSON behind them. A field season no longer lives across six laptops and a dozen phones — it lives in one place the whole team can trust, search, and add to from anywhere.

02

The story engine

Select a handful of items straight from the vault — a dive photo, a temperature graph, a paragraph from the field notes — and hand them to Polaris's content engine. It reads every format at once and drafts a social thread, a press release, or a full blog post that cites the exact evidence behind each claim, so a researcher edits a draft instead of starting from a blank page.

03

The learning bridge

Alongside every published expedition, Polaris keeps a companion shelf built for classrooms — lesson-ready explainers, annotated photos, and short clips a teacher can drop straight into a lesson plan. Students can follow an active expedition as it happens instead of reading about one that's long over, and teachers get material they didn't have to build from scratch.
